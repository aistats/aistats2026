# Visa & Travel Information

The conference took place in Tangier, Morocco. The notes below are general guidance only. Visa requirements depend on nationality and may change; always verify before travel.

## Visa-exempt entry (typical short stays)

Nationals of many countries may enter Morocco without a visa for short stays, including EU / Schengen Area countries, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, GCC countries, Turkey, and others.

## Visa required prior to travel

Participants from many African and Asian countries generally need a visa before travel (examples include India, Nigeria, Egypt, Pakistan—confirm for your nationality).
