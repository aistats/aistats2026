# Visa & Travel Information

The conference will take place in Tangier, Morocco. The information below is provided as general guidance only. Visa requirements depend on nationality, passport type, and length of stay and may change at any time. **Please verify your requirements before traveling.**

## Visa-exempt entry

Short stays · typically up to 90 days

Nationals of many countries may enter Morocco without a visa for short stays, including:

- EU / Schengen Area countries
- United Kingdom
- United States
- Canada
- GCC countries
- Turkey
- …and several other countries

## Visa required prior to travel

Application must be completed before arrival

Participants holding passports from most African and Asian countries are generally required to obtain a visa before traveling to Morocco.

**Examples (non-exhaustive):** India, Nigeria, Egypt, Pakistan, and others. Always confirm requirements for your nationality.
