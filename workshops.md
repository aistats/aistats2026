# Workshops

AISTATS 2026 ran an inaugural workshop programme on **May 5, 2026**, adjacent to the main conference (immediately after the main programme on May 2–4).

Workshops are interactive forums for emerging questions, open problems, and cross-disciplinary directions. Formats prioritise participation—contributed content, panels, and time for posters and discussion.

## Call timeline

* Submission deadline: **Friday, October 17, 2025 AOE**
* Decision notification: Mid-December 2025
* Workshop day: **May 5, 2026**

Suggested dates for accepted workshop organisers: workshop contribution deadline late January; accept/reject notifications early March.

## Submission format

Proposals were submitted through OpenReview as a **3-page** PDF (excluding references) in the AISTATS 2026 paper format, covering motivation, related events, potential invited speakers/panelists, tentative schedule (typically 9am–5pm), and short organiser biographies.

For the live workshop schedule and abstracts, see the [virtual workshop pages](https://virtual.aistats.org/virtual/2026/events/workshop). Canonical call: [Call for Workshops](https://virtual.aistats.org/Conferences/2026/CallForWorkshops).
