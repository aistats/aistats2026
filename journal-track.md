# Journal-to-Conference Track

The second edition of the Journal-to-Conference track at AISTATS invited authors of papers published in, or accepted by, selected journals to present their work as posters at AISTATS 2026, following the successful pilot in 2025.

## Eligibility

Eligible papers must:

* Have been published or accepted after **January 1, 2024** in one of:
  * Annals of Statistics
  * Biometrika
  * Journal of the American Statistical Association
  * Journal of Machine Learning Research
  * Journal of the Royal Statistical Society Series B
* Not contain contributions already published at a conference with archival proceedings by the same authors (the journal paper cannot be an extension of a previous conference publication)
* Not have been presented at a previous AISTATS Journal-to-Conference track
* Not have been presented, nor be scheduled for presentation, at Journal-to-Conference tracks of sister AI/ML conferences (for example NeurIPS, ICLR, ICML)

Authors must supply a working link to the journal website confirming publication or acceptance.

## Applying

Requests were submitted via [this form](https://forms.gle/KXFzzdutGGwVjw3i9). The deadline was **January 31, 2026 AOE**. Decisions were due by email no later than **February 15, 2026 AOE**.

No additional peer review was conducted. If submissions exceeded venue capacity, priority was given to early-career authors, diversity goals, and researchers without another AISTATS 2026 paper.
