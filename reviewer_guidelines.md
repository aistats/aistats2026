# Reviewer Guidelines

Guidelines for AISTATS 2026 reviewers. The full review form, FAQs, and updates are maintained on the virtual site:

**[Reviewer Guidelines (canonical)](https://virtual.aistats.org/Conferences/2026/ReviewerGuidelines)**

## Responsibilities

* Report problematic papers to the Area Chair (formatting issues, dual submissions, plagiarism, and similar)
* Submit high-quality reviews
* Discuss with other reviewers and the AC during the rebuttal / discussion period
* Recommend papers for oral talks and awards where appropriate
