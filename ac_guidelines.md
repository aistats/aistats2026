# Area Chair (AC) Guidelines

Guidelines for AISTATS 2026 Area Chairs. Full detail and FAQs:

**[AC Guidelines (canonical)](https://virtual.aistats.org/Conferences/2026/ACGuidelines)**

## Responsibilities

* Identify desk-rejection cases
* Help reviewer assignments and adjust loads
* Monitor the review process and reviewer responsiveness
* Organise reviewer discussions after author feedback
* Make accept/reject recommendations
* Recommend accepted submissions for oral talks
* Resolve issues during review (emergency reviewers, concerns from reviewers/authors)
