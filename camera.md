# Instructions for Camera-Ready Paper

For a camera-ready paper to be included in the proceedings, follow these instructions in full. Canonical details: [virtual camera-ready page](https://virtual.aistats.org/Conferences/2026/CameraReadyInstructions).

## TL;DR

* Camera-ready deadline: **Tuesday, March 31, 2026, Anywhere on Earth (AoE)** (strict)
* Submit on OpenReview from your paper page: **Edit → Camera Ready Revision**
* Upload:
  * One PDF with the **de-anonymized** paper (mandatory)
  * One PDF filled and signed copyright form (mandatory)
  * One URL to a GitHub repository with code/datasets (optional)

## Deadline flexibility

Authors unable to complete camera-ready submissions on time due to exceptional circumstances (for example disrupted internet access) should contact [aistats2026@gmail.com](mailto:aistats2026@gmail.com). The organising committee will make every reasonable effort to find an appropriate way forward.

## Manuscript requirements

* Maximum length **9 pages** (excluding acknowledgments, references, and optional appendix). Use the extra page to address reviewer feedback.
* De-anonymize with `\usepackage[accepted]{aistats2026}` from the [paper pack]({{ "/AISTATS2026PaperPack.zip" | relative_url }}).
* Main paper and appendix in **one PDF**, not exceeding 10MB.
* Title in title case; do not change the paper title without contacting the publication chair if needed.

See also [poster instructions]({{ "poster.html" | relative_url }}) and [submission FAQs]({{ "faqs.html" | relative_url }}).
