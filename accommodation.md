# Hotel Information

AISTATS 2026 took place at the **Hilton Tangier Al Houara Resort & Spa** in Tangier, Morocco. Conference dates were May 2–5, 2026 (hotel blocks May 1–6).

Official booking information, overflow hotels, and transport notes are on the virtual site:

**[Hotel Information & Reservations](https://virtual.aistats.org/Conferences/2026/Hotels)**

Overflow properties announced there included Hilton Tanger City Center and Hilton Garden Inn, with transport to the main venue.
