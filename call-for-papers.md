# Call for Papers

We invite submissions to the 29th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Statistics (AISTATS 2026) and welcome paper submissions at the intersection of artificial intelligence, machine learning, statistics, and related areas. Accepted papers will be presented at the conference held in person in Tangier, Morocco on May 2–5, 2026. At least one author of each accepted paper should register and present the work at the conference. Exceptions may be granted in case of travel emergencies or visa issues.

AISTATS is an interdisciplinary gathering of researchers from computer science, artificial intelligence, machine learning, statistics, and related areas. Since its inception in 1985, the primary goal of AISTATS has been to broaden research in these fields by promoting the exchange of ideas among them. The conference is committed to diversity in all its forms and encourages submissions from authors of underrepresented groups and geographies in ML/AI.

## Paper Submission (Proceedings Track)

The proceedings track is the standard AISTATS paper submission track. There is a separate [Journal-to-Conference track]({{ "journal-track.html" | relative_url }}) for papers recently published at selected journals.

Papers are selected via a rigorous double-blind peer-review process. Acceptance rates tend to be around 25%. All accepted papers are presented at the conference as posters, with a subset presented as talks, and are published in the AISTATS Conference Proceedings as part of the Proceedings of Machine Learning Research (PMLR). Papers for talks and posters are treated equally in publication.

Solicited topics include, but are not limited to:

* Machine learning methods and algorithms (classification, regression, unsupervised and semi-supervised learning, clustering, logic programming, …)
* Probabilistic methods (Bayesian methods, approximate inference, density estimation, tractable probabilistic models, probabilistic programming, …)
* Theory of machine learning and statistics (optimization, computational learning theory, decision theory, online learning and bandits, game theory, frequentist statistics, information theory, …)
* Deep learning (theory, architectures, generative models, optimization for neural networks, …)
* Reinforcement learning (theory of RL, offline/online RL, deep RL, multi-agent RL, …)
* Ethical and trustworthy machine learning (causality, fairness, interpretability, privacy, robustness, safety, …)
* Applications of machine learning and statistics (including natural language, signal processing, computer vision, physical sciences, social sciences, sustainability and climate, healthcare, …)

## Changes of Title / Abstract / Authorship

The author list at the *abstract* submission deadline is considered final, and no changes in authorship are allowed. This avoids new unforeseen conflicts of interest after bidding, which starts immediately after the abstract deadline. Author order may be changed after acceptance. Submissions violating these rules may be deleted after the paper deadline without review. Major changes to the title and abstract after the abstract deadline may be flagged for desk rejection after review.

## Formatting and Supplementary Material

Submissions are limited to **8 pages** (excluding references, the reproducibility checklist, and additional appendices) using the LaTeX style in the [AISTATS 2026 paper pack]({{ "/AISTATS2026PaperPack.zip" | relative_url }}) (9 pages for camera-ready). Pages containing only citations, the reproducibility checklist, and appendices are not limited.

Additional supplementary material must be submitted separately in a single PDF or a ZIP for other formats. Supplementary content may instead be appended after the references and reproducibility checklist in the main PDF. Supplementary material must not conflict with the main paper (for example, it must not be an updated version of an appendix already in the manuscript).

Submissions were accepted through OpenReview at [{{ site.conference.submission.url }}]({{ site.conference.submission.url }}). The site started accepting submissions on September 1, 2025.

## Reviewer Nomination

For each submission, the authors must nominate at least one of the authors as a reviewer for AISTATS 2026, who may be assigned up to 3 papers for review.

## Anonymization Requirements

The review process is double-blind. Submissions must be anonymized and must not contain information that violates double-blind reviewing (author names, affiliations, acknowledgements, or identity-revealing links). Self-citations are allowed if anonymity is preserved. Preprints must not be explicitly identified as an AISTATS submission during the review period.

## Dual Submissions

Submitted manuscripts should not have been previously published in a journal or conference proceedings, and should not be under consideration at another conference during the AISTATS review process. Extended abstracts (4 pages or less) at workshops or non-archival venues do not count as concurrent submissions. Tech reports on arXiv and short workshop papers without archival proceedings do not count as prior publication.

## Use of Generative AI

Authors may use generative AI tools to assist writing or research, but must take full responsibility for all content. LLMs are not eligible for authorship. Submitting a paper with a hidden prompt intended to obtain a favourable review from an LLM is scientific misconduct.
